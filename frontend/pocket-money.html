🔐 App security

Choose how to protect this app on this device
PIN / password lock

Set a 4–12 digit PIN or a password. It is stored as a salted hash in this browser.

Fingerprint / face unlock

Uses your device's platform authenticator when supported by your browser. Android may show fingerprint, face unlock or screen-lock verification.

Auto-lock

Lock after 1 minute without activity, or lock immediately.

Remove PIN/password

Disables the passcode lock. Biometrics can remain enabled.

Important: this is a browser-based app lock, not device encryption. Anyone with technical access to this browser profile may be able to inspect local data. Export a backup and protect your phone with Android screen lock.

Scan payment QR

Scan the receiver's UPI QR. The contribution amount will be filled in for you.

Before paying, verify the recipient name, UPI ID and amount in Google Pay. This app cannot verify a UPI payment automatically. Record the contribution only after Google Pay shows success.
